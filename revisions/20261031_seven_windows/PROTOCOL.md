# Frozen WorldMoE contract-window extension

This is a new evaluation dated 31 October 2026. It does not retrain or fit any model, replace any historical execution file, or look at realised future prices or PV when choosing a current action.

The three frozen Eagle checkpoints are 20260920/epoch06, 20260921/epoch02 and 20260922/epoch00. The evaluated 2025 admission path, source reference, electricity account, static import caps, unit service capacity, change charge and terminal valuation are the same as the recovered historical records. The new runs are 8 h, 20 h, 36 h and 48 h for each checkpoint, preceded by a repeated 24 h anchor for the shared implementation. Historical 12 h and 16 h points remain separately labelled as recovered historical results.

The neural model still receives its original 96-slot by 22-feature input. Its forecast lead coordinates and the 4/8/12/24 h backlog bins retain their original definition. Total backlog includes all obligations, including those beyond the 24 h neural prediction horizon. The service queue and continuation calculation span max(96, 4W) slots, with a maximum of 192 for 48 h rights. Every obligation remains in this queue and enters the prefix feasibility guard; the long-window implementation does not truncate distant work.

The first 96 planning slots use exactly the original neural scenario predictions, causal lagged physical observations and publication-aware information gates. Additional slots use a declared deterministic continuation: frozen pre-2024 zonal median RT prices [28.37, 41.01, 40.56, 45.96] USD/MWh, zero PV, the declared maximum site PUE, zero learned opportunity adjustment, and the same service capacity. A historical median is not an upper or lower future-price bound. The tail is a specified control rule, not a guaranteed conservative monetary estimate. Zero PV and maximum PUE are physical-stress assumptions only.

The original policy's year-end early-completion heuristic is retained. Its queue target is min(4W-1, N-1-t), and it serves known work as early as possible during the final hour. This target can be stricter than the contractual exclusive deadline t+4W. It is a policy choice that aids zero-tail completion; it does not redefine the contract. Independent service audits must use the original contractual deadline and the full stored book. All obligations are retained, and any residual work is stored in the explicit 8,192-slot tail. The 24 h implementation reduces to the archived runner's queue, neural inputs, forecast arrays, planning length and year-end rule.

Each run saves the raw yearly result, complete service and reference books, in-year compute/import/value/MTM arrays, daily settlement and a manifest with input, checkpoint and runner hashes. Logs are retained. Exact feasibility repairs, when subsequently supplied, must be separate numerical witnesses; they must not overwrite these new raw controller results.

The initial anchor processes started with an imprecise metadata sentence saying the queue retained actual deadlines. The executed source is preserved as `code/executed_runner_initial_anchor.py`. `code/annotate_anchor_metadata.py` adds an explicit metadata clarification after completion without changing any calculation, trajectory or original log. Later runs use the corrected wording directly.
