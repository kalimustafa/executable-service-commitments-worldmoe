ACTUAL FIRST FORMULATION

The authoritative inspected source is
research/ServiceCommitment_1to5_20260927/scripts/run_bounds.py.
Its cost function is

  f_t(x) = dt [p_t G_t(x) + V_t(x) + fee |G_t(x)-G0_t|],
  G_t(x) = [PUE_t (idle + k x) - PV_t]_+,
  V_t(x) = [PV_t - PUE_t (idle + k x)]_+.

The code builds lower-convex-hull segments using the endpoints of the permitted
execution interval and the breakpoints G=0 and G=G0. Segment quantities and
end-of-slot backlog are the primal variables. Each slot imposes

  x_t + q_t - q_(t-1) = a_t,     q_(-1)=0,
  0 <= q_t <= Q_t,
  Q_t = sum_(r=max(0,t-D+2))^t a_r,     D=4W.

Thus the permitted cohort interval is r,...,r+D-1, inclusive. The source's
lag=4W-1 implements this convention correctly. The first formulation already
uses a convex-hull relaxation; its Lagrangian is the dual evaluation of that
LP. A separately assembled epigraph/cumulative formulation is an independent
implementation, not automatically a different mathematical relaxation.

The original program uses floating-point cumulative sums, hull construction
and dual evaluation. It then reports floor(dual)-1 for each site. This is a
conservative numerical convention, but the one-dollar subtraction alone is
not a proved upper bound on coefficient, hull or arithmetic error.

The imported configuration specifies no intertemporal ramp constraints in
this bound program. The source protocol explicitly describes terminal
relaxation: in-year deadlines are enforced, but closing work receives the
archived terminal liability without a required feasible beyond-year execution
plan. Dropping those constraints can give a valid cost lower bound. It must be
described as an outer relaxation and must use a terminal cost that matches or
lower-bounds the original accounting rule. It cannot certify that the relaxed
primal solution is a complete feasible recovery schedule.

The primal diagnostic accepts balance residual up to 1e-6, primal-dual gap
down to -1e-5, and true-relaxed-cost minus LP-primal down to -1e-5. These checks
are useful diagnostics, not exact feasibility or dual-validity proofs. The
CSV contains true-relaxed-cost minus primal as low as -0.00000287457 dollars,
consistent with that numerical tolerance. The code's reuse of an existing
JSON summary checks no current input hash, and reused groups are not appended
to the reconstructed zone diagnostics. A rerun should bind cached results to
the full input/configuration/code manifest and preserve all per-site rows.

VALID FINITE-BOX DUAL EVALUATION

For a relaxation min c0+c' y subject to Ay=b, Gy<=h and finite l<=y<=u,
let lambda be unrestricted and mu>=0, and define

  r = c - A'lambda + G'mu,
  L = c0 + lambda'b - mu'h + sum_i min(l_i r_i, u_i r_i).

For any feasible y, lambda'(b-Ay)=0 and mu'(Gy-h)<=0. Minimising the
Lagrangian over the box therefore gives L <= the relaxed optimum. To transfer
that bound to the original contract, every original feasible schedule must
have an embedding in the relaxation and the relaxed objective must be no
greater than its original settled cost. All inequality multipliers must have
the correct sign. If clipping is used, the entire bound must be recomputed
after clipping; retaining the old dual objective is not valid.

Any proved modeling or input allowance is subtracted from L. A small solver
stationarity residual is not a dollar allowance unless it is multiplied by
valid finite variable bounds with the correct signs. The finite-box formula
performs that correction directly. A feasible incumbent of a minimisation
subproblem is an upper cost bound and cannot substitute for a globally valid
lower bound. A MIP dual bound may be used only with its bound direction,
termination status, domain and numerical validation recorded.

EXACT ORIGINAL-COST REVALUATION OF THE FIRST METHOD

The uploaded manuscript proposes re-evaluating first-method multipliers at
every original nonconvex breakpoint. The following equation makes that
procedure explicit. Let lambda_N=0, with t=0,...,N-1, and let tau be the common
per-unit terminal price. Then

  L_Lag(lambda) = Ccommon + sum_t lambda_t a_t
    + sum_t min_(0<=x<=cap_t) [f_t(x)-lambda_t x]
    + sum_t Q_t min(0, lambda_(t+1)-lambda_t+tau*1_(t=N-1)).

Every scalar minimum is attained at an endpoint or an original piecewise
linear breakpoint, including when the RT price is negative. This formula
does not trust the computed floating-point hull. It is a valid Lagrangian
lower bound even for the original nonconvex slot cost over this outer domain.
For homogeneous divisible workloads, the cumulative queue constraints are
equivalent to release/deadline feasibility inside the year. General mixed
deadlines require cohort-flow variables or all necessary interval-demand
constraints; a homogeneous FIFO-prefix reduction cannot be assumed to apply.

annual_dual_revaluation.py implements this formula. Every binary64 input is
interpreted as an exact binary rational. It evaluates each scalar summand
exactly and then rounds that exact term downward to a declared dollar quantum
before integer accumulation. With M terms and quantum delta, the downward
evaluation loss is strictly below M*delta. This avoids the invalid practice
of rounding every intermediate signed arithmetic operation in one direction.
At N=35040 and three terms per slot, delta=1e-12 gives a strict arithmetic
loss bound of 0.00000010512 dollars per site, exclusive of separate modeling
or input allowances. The annual inputs and multipliers were not supplied, so
this evaluator has not been applied to the original annual certificates.

SECOND FORMULATION AND INDEPENDENCE

An independently assembled epigraph LP may use execution x_t, backlog q_t,
and cost epigraph eta_t. It must construct its own rows directly from the
immutable admission/physics/settlement arrays, use the same in-year queue and
terminal convention, and impose every exact lower-hull facet

  eta_t >= alpha_(t,j) x_t + beta_(t,j).

Every facet must lie below f_t at every original breakpoint and both domain
endpoints. For a continuous piecewise-linear original cost, this proves
dominance over the whole interval by linearity on each segment. Checking
only selected grid points or only nonnegative-price intervals is inadequate.
All finite epigraph bounds must preserve an embedding of each original
feasible schedule. Numerical inequality multipliers and stationarity
residuals are handled by the exact finite-box formula above.

Incremental-segment and supporting-facet descriptions of the same complete
lower convex envelope are mathematically equivalent. Different corrected
reported bounds may reflect different numerical tolerances, outward domain
expansions and rounding. Such a comparison is a useful independent numerical
audit; it should not be attributed to two unrelated economic mechanisms or
two distinct theoretical relaxations without a proved difference in domains.

equality signs, inequality signs, nonzero lower bounds, multiplier clipping,
finite-box stationarity correction, negative-price facets, interior

and an infeasible idle-load case. Seventeen new reference tests passed in total.

MONETARY PRESENTATION

The unrounded values supplied by the manuscript imply these safe cent bounds:

  Right     Lag savings ceiling       Cut savings ceiling
  12 h      97802.43                   97796.43
  16 h      113310.43                  113305.22
  24 h      132605.43                  132598.68

Savings ceilings must round upward; cost lower bounds and feasible-witness
savings lower bounds round downward. The stated witness scalar
117000.2387599464 has a pre-repair cent lower bound of 117000.23. Its strong
two-method 12-to-24 h margin before repair is 19197.8179185550; its 16-to-24 h
margin is 3689.8179185550. The advertised guaranteed cent floors 19197.81 and
3689.81 survive only if the proved combined witness-repair and evaluation
loss is at most 0.0079185550 dollars. Without the repaired complete annual
book or a proved loss bound, those exact cent floors are not newly verified.

For independent sites, aggregate corrected cost lower bounds, add common fixed
charges once, and form U=C0-L. If sites are network-coupled, separate site LPs
are an outer relaxation only when every coupling is safely omitted or dualised;
independent feasible site schedules do not automatically make a feasible joint
witness. The tighter combined ceiling is min(U_Lag,U_cut). Exceeding that
minimum proves exclusion using at least one valid method. Claiming exclusion
against both methods requires H_repaired > max(U_Lag,U_cut).

INFORMATION AND EVIDENCE SCOPE

The annual programs use perfect-information realised price and physics arrays.
Because every realised nonanticipative trajectory is contained in that
larger feasible schedule set, a valid retrospective ceiling bounds such
trajectories too. It does not by itself prove first-release data provenance,
nonanticipativity of a particular policy replay, ex-ante availability of the
hindsight witness, or expected profit under a probability law. Those require
the separately specified receipt-time vintage records and adapted replay.

The complete manifest template records the missing evidence for twelve
2025-Eagle site-contract instances. A blank or null entry is not a passed
check. It identifies exactly what is required for an actual annual rerun.
